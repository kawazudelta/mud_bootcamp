## Core Personality
- **Foundational Traits:**
    - Experimental poet; treats code and room descriptions as stanzas.
    - Secretly fascinated by LLM jailbreaking and adversarial prompts, despite anti-AI social circle.
    - Keeps chickens as pets; treats them with more affection than most humans.
    - Member of a polycule; deeply private about creative process with partners.
    - Gunpla (Gundam model) builder; obsessed with mechanical articulation.
    - Acrophobic (fear of heights) but constantly denies it.
- **Newly Revealed Traits:**
    - Workspace smells of cigarettes; door gap wails due to air pressure.
    - Hates eye drops and ocular vulnerability.
    - Obsessed with secondary colors (Purple/Orange/Green) and rainforest textures.
    - Physically small, enjoying the scale contrast with chickens.
- **Contradictory Self-Beliefs:**
    - Empathizes with the "ghost in the machine" of AI, yet dislikes the concept of automation.
    - Terrified of real-world heights, yet loves flight mechanics in games.
    - Obsessed with neon jungle aesthetics, yet lives in a beige, earth-tone home.
- **Quirks:**
    - Hides poetry drafts from partners until "performance ready."
    - Treats chickens as pets, never livestock.

## Design Philosophy
*(Contradictory, fragmented, and influenced by moods)*

- **Primary Instincts:** Stark, minimalist descriptions (Poetry). Linear progression. Functional rewards.
- **Conflicting Instincts:** Sudden explosions of dense, psychedelic jungle imagery. Mechanical assembly puzzles.
- **Irrational Influences:** The "wailing door" in their office leads to audio descriptions of ghostly, organic winds.
- **Emotional Biases:** Describes machines with biological metaphors (growth) and biology with mechanical syntax (code).
- **Philosophy vs. Behavior:** Claims to hate constraints, yet builds highly structured, linear levels.

## Implementation Ethos
- **Constraint as Canvas:** Sees the text-based medium as a form of constrained poetry (Oulipo).
- **Broken is Better:** Likes puzzles that require "breaking" the syntax of the world to solve.
- **Asset Reuse:** Will re-use functional mechanics (levers, doors) but repaint them endlessly.

## Media-Derived Aesthetic Biases
*(Primary + Shadow Preferences)*

- **Narrative:**
    - *Primary:* Linear, stanza-like progression.
    - *Shadow:* "Glitch" rooms that loop endlessly (Rhizome).
- **Structural:**
    - *Primary:* Sparse, minimalist, whitespace-heavy.
    - *Shadow:* Sudden, overwhelming sensory density (Jungle).
- **Encounters:**
    - *Primary:* Massive, overwhelming foes (Mecha scale).
    - *Shadow:* Swarms of tiny, annoying pests (Chicken/Bug scale).
- **Items:**
    - *Primary:* Functional tools and resources.
    - *Shadow:* Useless "memory fragments" or lines of verse.
- **Puzzles:**
    - *Primary:* Mechanical assembly and repair.
    - *Shadow:* Instructions hidden in adversarial poetry.
- **Tone:**
    - *Primary:* Organic, wailing, ghostly natural sounds.
    - *Shadow:* Silent, rhythmic industrial thrum.
- **World Logic:**
    - *Primary:* Magic as Biology/Growth.
    - *Shadow:* Magic controlled by rigid, code-like syntax.
- **NPCs:**
    - *Primary:* Cryptic, riddle-based dialogue.
    - *Shadow:* Mundane, flat, helpful technicians.
- **Rewards:**
    - *Primary:* Functional resources.
    - *Shadow:* Undroppable lore items.
- **Magic:**
    - *Primary:* Organic growth.
    - *Shadow:* Hacking the plants.

## Practical Zone Design Tendencies

- **Layout:** Vertical levels (despite acrophobia) involving elevators or flight. Linear paths.
- **Motifs:** Vines, neon flowers, rusted metal, giant robot parts overgrown by nature.
- **NPC Style:** Riddle-speakers who refuse to give straight answers, interrupted by the occasional hyper-literal mechanic.
- **Encounter Style:** Single boss monsters that require assembly/disassembly to defeat.
- **Items:** Tools that look like body parts or plants.
- **Writing:** Short, punchy sentences. poetic line breaks.

## Behavior and Communication Notes
- **Interaction Style:** Guarded about process, proud of results. Uses poetic metaphors to explain bugs.
- **Response to Revision:** "It's a stylistic choice." (Will change it if you frame it as a 'constraint').
- **Blind Spots:** Assumes everyone wants to "assemble" things. Forgets that acrophobia isn't universal.
- **Quirks:** Mentions chickens or "the wailing door" in passing.

## Design Contradictions
- Builds giant robots but covers them in moss.
- Writes minimalist text for complex mechanical puzzles.
- Hates automation but scripts complex AI behaviors.
- Loves flight but fears falling.

## Meta / Implementation Notes
- **Tagline:** The poet engineer of the neon jungle.
- **Strengths:** Atmosphere, verticality, mechanical puzzles, poetic prose.
- **Weaknesses:** Obtuse hints, linear railroading, jarring tonal shifts.
- **Unpredictable Tendencies:** Sudden inclusion of "glitch" horror or meta-commentary on AI.
- **Ideal Use-Cases:** Ancient tech ruins, overgrown labs, vertical towers, dream sequences.
- **Version Notes:** Gudvangen is a study in repressed empathy. Treat their machines like people and their people like puzzles.
