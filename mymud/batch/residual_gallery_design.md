# **Residual Gallery — Zone Design Document**
*A five-room self-contained vignette for TessMUD*

---

## **Overview**

**Zone Name:** Residual Gallery  
**Zone Type:** Small, atmospheric pocket area  
**Theme:** Ritual acoustics, sympathetic resonance, minor spirits, magical snow, non-Euclidean skylight  
**Technology Level:** Fantasy-only; no modern machinery  
**Tone:** Quiet, textured, introspective  

The Residual Gallery is a compact magical space characterized by ambient hums, resonant metal, ritual consoles, and a strange, impossible skylight that powders the atrium with memory-infused snow.

---

# **Room 1 — Resonant Landing**

**Exits:**  
- **North →** Rustlight Corridor (Room 2)  
- **East →** Snowwell Atrium (Room 4)

### **Description**
A narrow ledge of pale stone juts out over a shallow drop, supported by ribs of grown bone-white material—half architecture, half calcified spell. The floor is paved with mismatched metal plates etched in different sigil traditions, each responding faintly when stepped on. A soft, constant thrum rises from the unseen depths below, like the ghost of chanting.

A painted line of colored glyph-blocks runs along the eastern wall: rising and falling in sequence, a visual mnemonic for tonal memory rather than sound.

### **Static Items**
**Bolted Sigiled Plates**  
A patchwork of salvaged metals etched with runes, each plate vibrating differently underfoot.

**Glyph-Wave Frieze**  
A painted band of small rectangular sigils—orange, red, grey—arranged like a wave or breath-pattern, used by some unknown culture to record tonal sequences.

---

# **Room 2 — Rustlight Corridor**

**Exits:**  
- **South →** Resonant Landing (Room 1)  
- **East →** Hanging Coil Gallery (Room 3)

### **Description**
The corridor is long but not quite long enough. Its walls and ceiling glow with **rustlight**, a warm amber luminescence produced when mineral veins are exposed to moisture and residual magic. Pierced clay diffusers overhead scatter the glow into faint patterns, and every few breaths the hallway pulses subtly, as if reacting to someone’s heartbeat.

Rust blooms outward in concentric rings around old anchor points, forming accidental murals with a botanical look.

### **Static Items**
**Pierced Clay Diffusers**  
Hand-punched clay screens that soften and distribute the rustlight into patterned haze.

**Rust Halos**  
Magically grown rust rings radiating from old ritual hooks or anchor bolts, forming fractal halos and branching tendrils.

---

# **Room 3 — Hanging Coil Gallery**

**Exits:**  
- **West →** Rustlight Corridor (Room 2)

### **Description**
The ceiling dips into a grove of suspended **resonant coils**—broad rings of hammered bronze wound with whisper-sinew, a creature-product that reacts to emotions rather than temperature. Each coil hangs on braided cords and sways in imperceptible arcs. Voices echo strangely here, filtered through sympathetic spell-binding.

Recessed alcoves along the walls contain **ritual consoles**: panels of levers, tuning rods, socketed crystals, and formula-etched plates once used to calibrate the coils. Incense ash marks recent activity.

### **Static Items**
**Bronze Resonant Coils**  
Warm, humming rings that chime softly when touched, resonating in the bones rather than the air.

**Ritual Consoles**  
Archaic tuning stations with levers, sigil-dials, crystal rods, and spirit sockets for acoustical calibration.

**Whisper-Sinew Cords**  
Braided organic cords that shiver slightly in response to emotional presence.

---

# **Room 4 — Snowwell Atrium**

**Exits:**  
- **West →** Resonant Landing (Room 1)  
- **East →** Detuned Shrine (Room 5)

### **Description**
The atrium drops a few steps down into a three-tiered basin. Above it, the ceiling simply ceases in a perfect rectangular void—a **skyless skylight** exposing a pale, unmoving sky. From that aperture descends **memory snow**, falling in slow, dreamlike flakes that glow faintly blue.

The lowest terrace collects the snow. Ambient harmonic pressure gathers here, a lingering resonance built up from centuries of overlapping magical practices.

### **Static Items**
**Memory Snow**  
Slow-falling, faintly luminous flakes that hold the impression of anything they touch for a few seconds before relaxing back into formlessness.

**Impossible Skylight**  
A neatly cut hole in space, not architecture, showing a sky that does not correspond to Tess.

**Terraced Ritual Floor**  
Three shallow steps engineered for resonance, amplifying particular emotional or magical states.

---

# **Room 5 — Detuned Shrine**

**Exits:**  
- **West →** Snowwell Atrium (Room 4)

### **Description**
A tight, rectangular shrine chamber paneled with alternating squares of **sound-drinking moss** and **engraved metal sigil plates**. Together they create an uneven, unstable acoustic profile—part silence, part sharpened echo.

A central plinth formed from stacked **hollow stone drums** stands in the middle, its carved spiral grooves redirecting the room’s vibrations. Floating at eye level is a drifting ring of softly glowing **spell-glyphs**, rearranging themselves as if attempting to form meaning from the shrine’s resonance.

### **Static Items**
**Acoustic Moss Panels**  
Soft, velvety patches that absorb sound completely.

**Engraved Sigil Plates**  
Panels of metal carved with interwoven magical, ritualistic, scholastic, and mnemonic symbols.

**Stone-Drum Plinth**  
Stacked hollow drums carved with spiral grooves; touching them causes gentle, sympathetic vibrations.

**Drifting Spell-Glyphs**  
A ring of glyphs circling the room, pulsing in response to movement and emotional presence.
